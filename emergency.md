In December 2025, the Police introduced special emergency telephone numbers for foreign tourists affected by adverse weather conditions. Following Cyclone Ditwah in late November 2025, which caused flooding and landslides, the tourism hotline 1912 operates 24/7 in coordination with police and disaster management authorities.

The main tourist areas near Colombo and on the south coast of Sri Lanka remain largely unaffected, while the most affected area is the Hill Country including Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Badulla.

**Lost Passport Procedure:**
If you lose your passport: Visit the Department of Immigration in Colombo for temporary travel documentation, report the incident to Tourist Police or local police (dial 119), obtain a police report for insurance claims, and contact your hotel or tour operator for immediate assistance.

**Natural Disaster Response:**
During natural disasters: Follow alerts from SLTDA and local authorities, stay in contact with your hotel or guide, evacuate if instructed and move to higher ground in coastal regions.

**Medical Emergency Best Practices:**
For medical emergencies: Dial 1990 for ambulance, visit the nearest hospital, contact your travel insurance provider as soon as possible. Emergency operators speak English widely, especially in tourist areas; give clear and specific location information with landmarks, stay calm and speak clearly.
