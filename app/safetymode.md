**Safety Tips the AI Should Provide:**

**Personal Safety:**
Key safety tips include: keeping valuables secure and out of sight especially in crowded places, using hotel safes for passports and large cash sums, avoiding walking alone at night in isolated areas on well-lit and populated streets.

**Transportation Safety:**
Avoid using public buses due to high risk of harassment and theft. Always agree on a price before getting in tuk-tuks, as prices are definitely something you can negotiate.

**Scam Awareness:**
Be highly vigilant as the risk of scams is significant, occurring most often in popular tourist areas like Colombo, Kandy, Galle, and beach resorts in the south. Drink spiking occurs in nightlife hubs like Hikkaduwa or Unawatuna bars, with locals sometimes targeting relaxed tourists with synthetic cannabis or sedatives.
