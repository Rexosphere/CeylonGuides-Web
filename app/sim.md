## **SIM Cards & Connectivity in Sri Lanka: Complete Tourist Guide 2025**

### **Network Providers Overview**

Sri Lanka has four major mobile network operators, with varying coverage and pricing:

**1. Dialog Axiata** (Market Leader - 50%+ share)
- Widest 4G/5G coverage nationwide with over 220 active 5G sites serving 1.5 million customers
- Best overall network experience according to Opensignal's 2025 report
- Most reliable for both urban and rural areas
- Premium pricing but worth it for comprehensive coverage

**2. SLT-Mobitel** (Second largest - 28% market share)
- Fastest network speeds in Sri Lanka
- Excellent rural coverage
- Good value for money with competitive tourist packages

**3. Hutch** (Third largest - 26% market share)
- Budget-friendly option with affordable data plans
- Good urban coverage, particularly in Colombo
- Coverage can be spotty in rural and remote areas
- Average download speed of 7.4 Mbps, lower than Dialog and Mobitel

**4. Airtel**
- Dialog Axiata is the licensee of the Airtel brand in Sri Lanka
- Mainly covers cities and urban areas
- Limited rural coverage

### **Tourist SIM Card Options & Pricing (2025)**

**Dialog Tourist Plans:**
Three tourist-specific plans with 30-day validity:
- Plan 1: 20 GB data + Rs. 174 call credit for Rs. 1,399 (~$4 USD)
- Plan 2: 30 GB data + Rs. 164 call credit for Rs. 1,799 (~$5 USD)  
- Plan 3: 50 GB data + Rs. 250 call credit for Rs. 2,450 (~$7 USD)

**Mobitel Tourist Plans:**
Three options for different durations:
- 7-Day Plan: 15 GB data + unlimited local calls + 1,000 SMS for Rs. 821 (~$3 USD)
- 30-Day Plan: 30 GB data for Rs. 1,540 (~$5 USD)

**Hutch Tourist Plans:**
Tourist SIM for Rs. 999 includes 10GB data, 500 local minutes, and 50 international minutes, valid for 30 days

### **Where to Buy**

**At Airports:**
- Dialog Airport Store at the Arrival lobby at Bandaranaike International Airport
- Mobitel available at Bandaranaike, Mattala Rajapaksa, and Jaffna International Airports
- Hutch available at Andaranaike and Mattala Rajapaksa International Airports
- Note: Airport prices may be slightly higher than city stores

**In Cities:**
- Dialog, Mobitel, and Hutch retail stores and service centers
- Popular retail chains like Singer, Softlogic, and Keells supermarkets
- Authorized dealers and retailers

**Requirements:**
- Valid passport (mandatory for SIM registration)
- Photo may be taken if you don't have a passport-sized photograph

### **eSIM Options**

Both Dialog and Mobitel offer eSIM services:

**Dialog eSIM:**
- Can be purchased online before arrival
- Visit Dialog eSIM portal, complete verification, choose plan, and activate upon landing

**Mobitel eSIM:**
- Can request eSIM at Arrival lobby or any SLT Mobitel Outlet
- Available before you arrive in Sri Lanka

**Hutch eSIM:**
Two prepaid packages available:
- 125 GB data + unlimited local calling + Rs. 500 IDD calling for $10 USD (30 days)
- 50 GB data + unlimited local calling + Rs. 200 IDD calling for $7 USD (14 days)

### **5G Coverage & Network Speed**

**5G Availability:**
- Dialog launched commercial 5G service in December 2024 with 220 live sites and plans to invest $100 million over two years
- Dialog uses both 3500 MHz spectrum for wide-area coverage and 27 GHz spectrum for gigabit speeds
- SLT Mobitel also has 5G trials available
- 5G primarily available in Colombo with expanding coverage

**Network Performance:**
- As of late 2024, Dialog and SLT Mobitel lead in overall network experience
- 4G widely available in tourist areas
- Reliable connectivity in cities and major tourist destinations

### **Coverage by Region**

Coverage breakdown by area:
- Colombo & Western Province: All four networks provide excellent 4G
- Central Highlands (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella): Dialog and Mobitel most reliable
- Southern Coast (Galle, Mirissa, Unawatuna): All networks offer good coverage
- East Coast (Trincomalee, Batticaloa): Dialog provides most comprehensive coverage
- Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa): Dialog and Mobitel reliable at major sites

### **Wi-Fi Availability**

### **Digital Nomad Visa**

**Status (2025):**
Officially announced in 2021, approved by Cabinet of Ministers, appears to have launched during 2024

**Key Requirements:**
To qualify applicants must:
- Work remotely as employee, freelancer, or business owner with clients outside Sri Lanka
- Prove minimum monthly income of $2,000 channeled through Sri Lankan banking system
- Have valid passport with at least 6 months validity
- Provide travel insurance valid for entire stay

**Benefits:**
Valid for one year with possibility of renewal, costs $500 and covers spouse and dependents

**Alternative:**
Tourist visa can be extended up to 10 years with 180 days per year in the country

- Consider eSIM for convenience if your device supports it
- Purchase SIM in city for better deals than airport
